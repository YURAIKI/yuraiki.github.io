Data Deletion

Last updated November 10, 2026

VidLens does not require an account. Follow the steps below to manage analysis records and exported files on your device.

1. Delete an analysis or selected scenes

  • To delete an analysis, find it under Recent projects on the home screen, tap its delete button, and confirm.
  • To delete individual scenes, select them on the results screen, tap Delete selected, and confirm.

2. Clear app data and manage backups

To clear all VidLens app data from your device, use Settings → Apps → VidLens → Storage → Clear storage or uninstall the app. Menu names vary by device. Android may later restore records included in a backup. Use your device's backup settings to manage backup copies separately.

3. Original videos and exported files

Deleting analysis records leaves original videos and previously exported JPG, PDF, and SRT files in place. To delete exports, open Download/Vidlens in your file manager. To delete an original video or a copy shared with another service, delete it where it is stored.

4. Support and advertising data

To request deletion of support information you sent to YURAIKI, email support@yuraiki.kr. We will review the request and tell you the outcome. We keep records that the law requires us to retain for the applicable period. To manage advertising data handled by Google, use Ad privacy settings when it appears in the app, Android ad settings, or your Google Data & Privacy settings.
