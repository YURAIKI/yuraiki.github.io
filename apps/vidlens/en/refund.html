Payments and Refunds

Last updated November 10, 2026

1. Current pricing

The current Android version of VidLens is free and offers no in-app purchases, paid subscriptions, or automatic renewals. VidLens does not charge a fee for the current app features, so there is no VidLens payment to refund for their use. Your carrier may charge for data used to load ads or download language models.

2. Rewarded ads

Precise analysis and lossless PDF export are available after you complete the ad described in the app. If you cannot complete the ad, you can use the standard options shown in the app, such as standard analysis or high-quality PDF export.

3. Future paid features

If we introduce paid features, we will disclose pricing, access periods, automatic renewal terms, and cancellation and refund instructions before purchase. Cancellations and refunds will follow applicable law and payment platform rules. Your statutory cancellation and refund rights apply.

4. Contact

If a feature is unavailable after you complete an ad or you see a charge related to VidLens, contact support@yuraiki.kr. Include your app version and a description. If you are asking about a charge, include the order number if needed. Keep card numbers and account passwords out of your message.
