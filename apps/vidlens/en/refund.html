Payments and Refunds

Last updated November 11, 2026

1. Premium plans and payment

VidLens's basic features are free. Premium purchases supported from VidLens 0.4 are processed by Google Play. Available plans and the actual price appear in the app's Premium screen and at Google Play checkout.

  • Weekly subscription renews automatically each week until canceled. You are charged each billing cycle.
  • Lifetime access unlocks Premium features with a single payment.

2. Manage a subscription or restore a purchase

Use the Google Account you purchased with to manage or cancel your weekly plan in Google Play subscriptions. Canceling stops the next renewal. You can continue using Premium for the time you have already paid for. Uninstalling the app leaves your subscription active.

If you reinstall VidLens or switch devices, select the Google Account you purchased with and choose Premium → Restore purchases to recover lifetime access or an active subscription.

3. Refunds and unauthorized charges

Refunds are handled under applicable law and Google Play policies. See the Google Play refund guide for request instructions. Report unauthorized charges through Google Play's unrecognized charge form.

4. Purchase and access support

If you have trouble restoring a purchase or using Premium, email support@yuraiki.kr with your app version and a description of the issue. Include your Google Play order number if needed to identify the transaction. Keep card numbers and account passwords out of your message.
