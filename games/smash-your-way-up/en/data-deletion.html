Data Deletion

Hammer Go! · Version 1 · Effective October 25, 2026

Delete local progress

The current game has no separate game account or operator-hosted progress sync. In Android Settings, select the app and clear its storage/data to remove local scores, coins, items, settings and acceptance records. Menu names vary. Consider the possible loss of progress before deleting.

Backups and exported files

Android backups and device-transfer copies must be managed separately through the platform. Exported XML, PNG or ZIP files and shared copies are not removed by uninstalling the app; delete them from their file locations and shared storage.

Support and advertising data

Request deletion of information sent to support at support@yuraiki.kr. Only necessary verification is requested, and legally required retention will be explained.

For information Google processes in advertising services, also see Google’s management and deletion procedures. You can change advertising privacy choices in game Settings where applicable.
