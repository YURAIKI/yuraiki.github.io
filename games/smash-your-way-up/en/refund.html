Payments and Refunds

Hammer Go! · Version 1 · Effective October 25, 2026

Current purchases

Character, outfit and equipment purchases currently use earned game coins, not real-money payments. Displaying an ad-removal product does not charge you without a separate platform purchase approval. Google Play Billing is not connected for real-money purchases in this version.

If paid products are introduced

Prices, contents, duration, delivery, renewal and withdrawal information will be shown before payment. Cancellation and refunds follow applicable law and payment-platform policies. This notice does not remove statutory refund rights or promise purchase restoration, subscriptions or refund features that are not currently available.

Report a problem

For coin deductions or missing items, contact support@yuraiki.kr with a description, game version and, if helpful, a screenshot. If an actual payment issue arises, an order number may help. Never send card numbers or account passwords.
